Terms of Service

Last updated: 5 September 2026

1. What this is

MovieClipsFactory is an application that runs on the computer of the person using it. It cuts long videos into vertical clips, generates captions and narration, and — when asked — sends those clips to that person's own social accounts.

2. It is not a service

There is no server, no account, no subscription and no guaranteed availability. The program runs locally. If it does not work, nobody is on call.

3. Rights in the material

You are responsible for the material you import. The application does not verify that you have the right to use a video, and does not assume that you do: a rights gate requires an explicit declaration before any publication, and it blocks by default.

Use public-domain material, material licensed for your use, or material of your own. Publishing someone else's content without the right to do so violates the terms of the social platforms and the law, and the responsibility belongs to whoever publishes.

4. The connected accounts are yours

The application acts only on accounts you authorised through OAuth, and only when you ask. It does not post on its own: there is a master publishing switch and a separate switch for public posts, and both are off by default.

5. The platforms' rules apply

Connecting YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or Facebook leaves you subject to each one's terms. The application tries to respect them — it asks for per-post consent where the platform requires it, does not force a privacy setting the account does not offer, and does not publish a public Reel without a named authorisation — but it does not replace your obligation to know them.

On TikTok specifically, and as stated on the home page, this kind of tool is not eligible for audited Content Posting API access. The application uses the unaudited draft path and does not represent itself as something else.

6. No warranty

The application is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. Whoever uses it assumes the risk of that use, including the risk of an unwanted publication.

This text may change when the application changes. The date at the top says when it last did.
