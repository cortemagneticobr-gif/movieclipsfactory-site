Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

The short version

MovieClipsFactory runs on your computer. It has no server, no user account and no analytics. Nobody — including whoever wrote it — receives your videos, your tokens or any record of what you did with it.

What stays on your computer

  • The videos you import, the clips produced, the captions and the audio.
  • The local database with your projects, clips and history.
  • The access tokens of any social account you connect.

Tokens are written encrypted by the Windows DPAPI, the same protection the operating system uses for application credentials. They are not written in plain text, do not appear in logs, are not placed in configuration files and are not committed to source control. Only your Windows user, on that computer, can decrypt them.

What leaves your computer, and where it goes

Nothing leaves without an action of yours.

There is no telemetry

The application sends no usage statistics, reports no errors anywhere, and has no analytics. The module named telemetry in its source measures the memory of its own process, on your machine.

What it reads from the platforms

The minimum needed to work: the account identifier, the display name, and the status of the publications you yourself created. None of it is shared with anyone, because there is nobody to share it with.

The platforms have their own policies

Once you publish to a network, the content is governed by that network's terms and privacy policy. MovieClipsFactory does not change that and has no control over what they do afterwards.

Children

The application is not directed at children and collects nothing about anyone, of any age.

Changes

This text changes when the application changes. The date at the top says when it last did.
